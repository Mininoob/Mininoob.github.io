Module 01 · deployed · CACI International · 2024–present

        
Medical Triage Simulator

        
I design and maintain core features of a triage simulator that trains medical personnel and supports research studies. Performance is up 50% since I started on it. I also rebuilt the telemetry path with redundant writes, so no experiment loses its data.

      

      

        
Module 02 · fielded · CACI International · internship · 2022–2024

        
ACND

        
A real-time cyber defense dashboard that pulls several data sources into a single view so responders can move fast. Behind it sits an AI model we trained to catch and mitigate attacks on HMI, SCADA, and PLC infrastructure while they are happening.

      

      

        
Module 03 · fielded · CACI International · internship · 2022–2024

        
Cityblock

        
A digital-twin platform that spins up virtual environments for penetration testing and security evaluation. It emulates real hardware with virtual machines, or links to live equipment and streams its data into the twin, so what you test matches what is actually running.

A HoloLens tool for wildfire response. It pulls real GIS terrain, vegetation, and weather data, then runs a plume simulation to project where a fire spreads next. Crews get to see the path before the fire takes it.

A Lego environment captured by photogrammetry and rebuilt in Unity 3D, with live PLC data synced into the virtual scene. Built with Port San Antonio to get students interested in engineering.

      

      

        
Module 06 · research · UTSA SAVE Lab · 2022–2023

        
VR Cybersickness Prediction

        
Two pieces of work. First the dataset: participants walked a procedurally generated VR maze for ten minutes while we recorded gaze, heart rate, and other signals. Then the model trained on it, which reached over 90% accuracy on cognitive load and attention.

Autonomous LiDAR Rover + Drone

        
A fully autonomous platform that pairs a 3D-printed rocker-bogie rover with a drone for LiDAR terrain mapping. Point-cloud processing runs onboard in ROS.

Part 03 · personal bays

      
Off-mission builds

      
Personal projects, built off the clock: a game engine, a notes app I use every day, and a product platform that shipped.

    


    

      

        
Bay P-01 · in development · C++20 · Vulkan 1.3

        
Polymage

        
A low-poly mage action-RPG running on a C++20 engine I wrote from scratch over Vulkan 1.3, cross-platform on Windows and macOS. The world is infinite, seed-based, and streams in around the player at 0.25 m resolution. It renders through a GPU compute-shader raymarcher rather than triangle meshes, which is where the caves, overhangs, and micro-voxel look come from. The 60 Hz ECS simulation (EnTT and Jolt) is deterministic and takes nothing but input command structs, so any run can be replayed and checked: CI replays a command stream on every commit and asserts the results come out identical. That also leaves the door open for networked multiplayer. On top of it runs the game itself, with procedurally generated biomes, dungeons, and towns, a data-driven spell system, loot and equipment, and boss fights that shift phases, track threat, and telegraph their heavy attacks.

      

      

        
Bay P-02 · operational · Rust · Tauri 2 · SolidJS

        
Pinq

        
A local-first sticky-note and knowledge app for Windows and macOS. Notes stay transparent and on top of everything, and you pull a new one out from the edge of the screen. Sync is offline-first, built on an append-only action log with version-vector deltas, and devices reach each other over end-to-end-encrypted peer-to-peer links that keep co-editing under a second. Tag and related-note suggestions come from an ONNX model running on the machine, so note content never leaves it.

      

      

        
Bay P-03 · shipped · SwiftUI · FastAPI · Docker

        
OriginalCopy

        
A photobooth platform I built and shipped end to end. The booth itself is a SwiftUI app doing live camera capture and thermal receipt printing through the Epson ePOS SDK. Behind it, a FastAPI backend handles device enrollment, media, telemetry, and multi-tenant auth across five roles, with claim and gallery pages for guests and a dashboard operators watch their booths from. It runs containerized behind Caddy and nginx, deployed by release with instant rollback.
