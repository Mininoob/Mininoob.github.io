Part 02 — payload bays

      
Simulation modules

      
Fielded builds and research platforms — simulators, digital twins, and autonomous systems from work at CACI, UTSA SAVE Lab, and NASA MIRO CAMEE.

    


    

      

        
Module 01 · deployed · CACI International · 2024–present

        
Medical Triage Simulator

        
Core feature work on a triage simulation platform used for live training of medical personnel and for research — performance improved by 50%, with hardened data logging so 100% of research data survives a session.

      

      

        
Module 02 · fielded · CACI International · internship · 2022–2024

        
ACND

        
Real-time cyber defense dashboard integrating multiple data sources for rapid incident response — with an AI model trained to detect and mitigate attacks on HMI, SCADA, and PLC infrastructure in real time.

      

      

        
Module 03 · fielded · CACI International · internship · 2022–2024

        
Cityblock

        
Digital-twin security platform that spins up virtual environments for simulation and penetration testing — emulating real hardware with virtual machines, or linking to live hardware and streaming its real-time data into the twin.

      

      

        
Module 04 · research · UTSA SAVE Lab × NASA MIRO CAMEE · 2022

        
Project Wildfire

        
A HoloLens firefighting tool that simulates wildfires from real GIS terrain, vegetation, and weather data — driving plume simulation to project a fire’s spread and path, so the teams fighting it can see what the fire will do next.

      

      

        
Module 05 · outreach · CACI × Port San Antonio · internship · 2022–2024

        
Lego Digital Twin

        
A live Lego environment captured through photogrammetry into Unity 3D, with real PLC data synced to the virtual scene — a working digital twin built to promote STEM education.

      

      

        
Module 06 · research · UTSA SAVE Lab · 2022–2023

        
VR Cybersickness Prediction

        
Cybersickness research in two parts: a dataset built by placing participants in a procedurally generated VR maze for 10-minute real-walking sessions — capturing gaze, heart rate, and more — and a predictive model trained on it reaching over 90% accuracy on cognitive and attention analysis.

      

      

        
Module 07 · research · NASA MIRO CAMEE · 2020–2022

        
Autonomous LiDAR Rover + Drone

        
A fully autonomous platform pairing a 3D-printed rocker-bogie rover with an autonomous drone for LiDAR terrain mapping and data acquisition — with onboard point-cloud processing in ROS.

      

      

        
Module 08 · research · NASA MIRO CAMEE · 2020–2022

        
V-RBNN Coastal Reconnaissance

        
Coastal reconnaissance research applying the V-RBNN algorithm to LiDAR point clouds with multi-sensor fusion — built in C++ and Python on ROS, optimizing data cleanliness to support coastal survey needs.

      

    


  



  
  

    

      
Part 03 — personal bays

      
Off-mission builds

      
Personal projects, built off the clock — a game engine, a daily-driver notes app, and a shipped product platform.

    


    

      

        
Bay P-01 · in development · C++ · Vulkan

        
Polymage

        
A custom game engine written in C++ on Vulkan, purpose-built to power a multiplayer open-world voxel mage game — players explore towns, cities, and dungeons, and team up with friends to take down bosses.

      

      

        
Bay P-02 · operational · Rust · Tauri 2 · SolidJS

        
Pinq

        
A local-first sticky-note & personal knowledge app for Windows and macOS — always-on-top transparent notes with a screen-edge pull-to-create gesture, an offline-first sync engine built on an append-only action log with version-vector deltas, and end-to-end-encrypted peer-to-peer sync with sub-second co-editing. On-device ONNX embeddings suggest tags and related notes — no note content ever leaves the machine.

      

      

        
Bay P-03 · shipped · SwiftUI · FastAPI · Docker

        
OriginalCopy

        
A photobooth platform built and shipped end to end — a SwiftUI booth app with live camera capture and Epson thermal receipt printing, a FastAPI backend handling device enrollment, media, telemetry, and multi-tenant auth across five roles, plus guest claim & gallery pages and a live operator dashboard. Runs containerized behind Caddy and nginx with release-based deploys and instant rollback.
